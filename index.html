Observing the sky to understand the Earth

Ancient Indian knowledge systems used observations of the Sun, Moon, stars and other celestial bodies to understand time, direction and position. Through astronomical observations, mathematical methods and instruments such as the Śaṅku, ancient scholars developed practical approaches to celestial mapping and geodetic positioning. These practices show how astronomy, mathematics and geography were interconnected in ancient India.
